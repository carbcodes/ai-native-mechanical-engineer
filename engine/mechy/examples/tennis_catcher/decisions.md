# Decisions
- 2026-10-23: v1 is a conventional belt-driven arm (`style: belt_arm`, after the 20sffactory Community Robot Arm / Thor pattern: all motors low, GT2 20T to printed pulleys on closed belts, 51105 + 608-2RS bearings) (why: the primitive-block v1 was rejected; standards rules 1-3) [evidence](design/v1/summary.md)
- 2026-10-23: elbow and wrist driven by belt pass-through (1:1 belts along the links), not a parallelogram (why: the wrist is actuated, and pass-through keeps the cup level when the wrist motor holds) [evidence](design/v1/summary.md)
- 2026-10-23: shoulder 7:1 (20T to 140T) instead of 5:1 (why: 5:1 gives only 0.89x the 50% torque budget with the arm stretched; 7:1 gives 1.13x and still reaches 257 deg/s) [evidence](design/v1/summary.md)
- 2026-10-23: drivers at 4 microsteps (why: the 4000 steps/s cap allows 5 rev/s per motor; 16 microsteps caps at 1.25 rev/s) [evidence](hw/drivers.yaml)
- 2026-10-23: cup liner is a slack net pocket, not EVA foam (why: the sim catches 39% with a liner restitution of ~0.40 and 91% at 0.15 (estimate); balls hitting the floor of a stiff liner bounce out. Drop-test the pocket before trusting it) [evidence](sim/catch-v1/summary.md)
- 2026-10-23: v2 = v1 + a drawn sprung cup (`compliance: spring`: 2 x 4 mm rods, LM4UU, 2 x RS PRO 821-352 springs = 220 N/m, 45 mm travel, felt friction brake ~3 N, hand_m 0.125) (why: sim cuts stepper skips on ball impact from 75% to 24% at 93.5% catch vs 92.5%; an undamped spring bounced the ball out, 31.5% catch, so the brake is required. Proposed; design/chosen is still v1) [evidence](design/v2/summary.md)
