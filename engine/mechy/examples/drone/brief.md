# Beginner 5-inch quadcopter

## Goal

A 3D-printable 5-inch quadcopter for a first FPV build: PETG frame, about 220 mm
motor-to-motor (diagonal) wheelbase, 4S battery. Parts must fit a normal 220 mm
printer bed, be easy to print (no supports), and cheap to replace after a crash.
The frame should leave room for a 30.5 mm stack (FC + 4-in-1 ESC) and a GoPro-size
battery on top or bottom.
