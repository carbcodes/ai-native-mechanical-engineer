# Decisions

- 5 inch props on 4S: the common beginner freestyle size; cheap, everywhere, easy to tune.
- PETG printed frame instead of carbon: printable at home, forgiving in crashes, heavier
  but fine for a first build.
- True-X layout, 220 mm wheelbase: equal front/back response, prop clearance on a small frame.
- 2306 2400KV motors + 4S 1300 mAh: standard mid-power 5-inch combo (estimates, verify on datasheet).
